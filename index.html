2. Permissions We Use

Permission Purpose
RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED Restore alarms after reboot.
SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM Trigger alarms at the exact scheduled time.
USE_EXACT_ALARM Ensure precise alarm scheduling.
USE_FULL_SCREEN_INTENT Display full-screen alarm.
POST_NOTIFICATIONS Show notifications and reminders.
VIBRATE Enable alarm vibration.
WAKE_LOCK Wake the device during alarms.
MODIFY_AUDIO_SETTINGS Control alarm sound and volume.
DISABLE_KEYGUARD Display alarm over lock screen.
FOREGROUND_SERVICE Reliable alarm execution.
FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK Play alarm sounds correctly.
REQUEST_IGNORE_BATTERY_OPTIMIZATIONS Improve alarm reliability.
CAMERA Capture photos for Secure Vault.
READ_MEDIA_IMAGES Import photos.
READ_MEDIA_VIDEO Import videos.
READ_MEDIA_AUDIO Import audio files.
READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE Access media on Android 12 and below.
WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE Store files on older Android devices.
MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE Manage user-selected files for Secure Vault functionality.
