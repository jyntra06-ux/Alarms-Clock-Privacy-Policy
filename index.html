2. Permissions We Use

Permission Purpose
RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED Restore scheduled alarms after the device is restarted.
SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM Schedule alarms to trigger at the exact time selected by the user.
USE_EXACT_ALARM Support precise alarm scheduling where applicable.
USE_FULL_SCREEN_INTENT Display the alarm screen when an alarm is triggered.
POST_NOTIFICATIONS Display alarm and reminder notifications.
VIBRATE Provide vibration when an alarm is triggered.
WAKE_LOCK Help keep the device active when necessary for alarm functionality.
MODIFY_AUDIO_SETTINGS Control audio settings required for alarm sounds.
DISABLE_KEYGUARD Allow the alarm interface to appear when the device is locked.
FOREGROUND_SERVICE Support reliable background operations required by the App.
FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK Support alarm audio playback when required.
REQUEST_IGNORE_BATTERY_OPTIMIZATIONS Help prevent battery optimization from interfering with reliable alarm operation.
CAMERA Allow users to capture photos using the device camera for Secure Vault functionality.

The App does not request broad device storage access or the MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE permission.
